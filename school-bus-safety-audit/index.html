What we check

Six areas that matter most to parents, principals and transport heads.

📍

GPS tracking & live location

Is every bus tracked? Can parents see live location and get arrival alerts?

📹

CCTV & dashcam

Are cameras working, covering the right areas, and is footage stored safely?

🪪

RFID boarding attendance

Does the school know which child got on and off which bus, and when?

🧑‍✈️

Driver & attendant checks

Are driver and attendant records, licences and contact details up to date?

🚨

SOS & emergency alerts

Can the driver or attendant raise an alert fast, and who gets it?

⏱️

Route timing & overspeed

Are routes on time? Are overspeed alerts set up and checked?

Questions schools ask

Anything else? Call or WhatsApp us.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The audit and the written report are free, and there is no obligation to buy anything.

Do we need any system already?

No. We check what you have today, even if you have no tracking or cameras yet.

Our buses are run by a vendor. Can you still audit?

Yes. We can check vendor buses with your permission and share the report with you.

How long does it take?

It depends on how many buses you run. We plan the visit around your school timings, and our team will confirm the time when we call.

Is the audit available in my city?

Call or WhatsApp us with your city and we will confirm.

What happens after the report?

We walk you through the findings. If you want help fixing the gaps, we can share a quote, but that is entirely your choice.
